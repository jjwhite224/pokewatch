WHAT COUNTS AS MSRP?

An exact product. A recorded source.

MSRP means the manufacturer's suggested retail price. We keep retailer-reported MSRP, official store prices, and your own limits clearly labeled.

A crossed-out store price alone doesn't count. Products without a reference stay out of price alerts.
